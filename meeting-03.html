Meeting 3 · TOEIC Part 2

Handling Offers, Invitations & Preferences

Respond to Yes/No, choice and tag questions, and make polite offers.

Language focus: three question types

TypeExampleHow to answer
Yes/NoIs the conference room available?Yes / No + detail: “No, it's booked until 4.”
Choice (A or B?)Would you like tea or coffee?Pick one. Never “yes” or “no”: “Coffee, please.”
TagYou're finished with the report, aren't you?Answer the fact: “Yes, I just sent it.”
Listen to the voice: in a choice question, the voice goes up on the first option and down on the last one.

Useful offer phrases

  • Would you like me to…?
  • Shall I…?
  • Can I give you a hand with…?
  • Why don't we…? · How about we…?
