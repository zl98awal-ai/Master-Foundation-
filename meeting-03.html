Meeting 3 · TOEIC Part 2

Handling Offers, Invitations & Preferences

Panduan mengajar · 90 menit · UIB 1TUMC, 1BAME, 1TUMM

Alur GBA Latihan kelas Kunci & intonasi File Drive
Communicative task
Making suggestions, offering help, choosing between workplace options
Target genre
Conversational offers, tag questions, choice questions
Grammar
Would you like…? / Why don't we…?; tag questions; choice questions with “or”
Vocabulary
Polite offers; confirming, agreeing and disagreeing

Alur GBA

BKOF15′

Phrase bank offers

  1. Tanya kelas: “What do you say when you want to offer help to a colleague?”
  2. Bangun dua kolom. Offering: Would you like me to…? · Shall I…? · Can I give you a hand with…? · I can take care of that if you'd like. Suggesting: Why don't we…? · How about we…? · Let's… · Maybe we could…
  3. Role-play berpasangan 30 detik, satu frasa dari tiap kolom.
MOT30′

Intonasi & tag questions

  • Choice: naik di opsi pertama, turun di opsi terakhir · Do you want to print this now (↗), or send it later (↘)? Contohkan berlebihan, kelas menirukan bersama.
  • Tag: positive → negative tag, negative → positive tag · The report is finished, isn't it? · You haven't submitted the form yet, have you?
  • Guided discrimination: 6 soal Yes/No, Choice atau Tag. Kunci: 1 Choice · 2 Tag · 3 Yes/No · 4 Choice · 5 Tag · 6 Yes/No.
JCA35′

Speed-networking

  1. Dua baris berhadapan. Tiap mahasiswa memegang satu query card (masalah kecil di kantor).
  2. Ronde 3′: S1 menyampaikan masalah; S2 menjawab dengan offer phrase + choice question (Would you like me to charge it for you, or shall we reschedule?).
  3. S1 mengonfirmasi dengan tag question (So you'll email me the file, won't you?). Rotasi 5–6 ronde (±20′).
  4. Debrief 10′: tampilkan percakapan terbaik atau frasa paling berguna.
ICA10′

TOEIC Part 2 check

12 soal Yes/No, Choice dan Tag. Intonasi harus tepat (lihat catatan di bawah), karena itu yang diuji.

Latihan kelas

Tayangkan di layar. Suara otomatis tidak selalu memberi intonasi choice yang tepat, jadi untuk soal choice lebih baik Bapak yang membacakan.
