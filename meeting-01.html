Meeting 1 · TOEIC Part 1

Describing Visual Surroundings & Physical Activities

Say what people are doing and where things are in a photograph.

Language focus

MeaningFormExample
Action: a person is doing somethingis / are + verb-ingThe man is operating a photocopier.
Action in progress: something is being done by a personis / are being + V3The monitors are being installed.
Position / state: no action, just where things areis / are + V3The chairs are arranged in a circle.
TOEIC tip: “is being + V3” is only true if you can see a person doing it. If the photo shows no people, “The boxes are being opened” is wrong, but “The boxes are stacked” can be right.

Photo traps to watch for

  • Wrong verb form: an action sentence for a photo with no action, or the other way round.
  • Wrong location: the right object, but in the wrong place.
  • Not in the photo: a person, object or action that you cannot see.

Practice: action or position?

Choose the meaning of each sentence. You will see the answer and a short explanation right away.
