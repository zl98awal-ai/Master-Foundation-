Describing Visual Surroundings & Physical Activities

Panduan mengajar · 90 menit · UIB 1TUMC, 1BAME, 1TUMM

Alur GBA Latihan kelas Naskah & kunci File Drive
Communicative task
Oral & written descriptions of ongoing actions in a campus/workplace setting
Target genre
Captioning photographs & descriptive reporting
Grammar
Present continuous (S + be + V-ing); passive positioning (S + be + V3)
Vocabulary
Active workplace verbs; location prepositions

Alur GBA

BKOF15′

Word bank dari foto

  1. Tayangkan 4–6 foto rutinitas kampus/kantor.
  2. Berpasangan, 4 menit: kumpulkan kata kerja aksi dan ungkapan lokasi.
  3. Tulis di papan: assembling, inspecting, filing, stacking, sorting, loading, typing… / in front of, next to, behind, between, on top of, against the wall…
  4. Drill lisan: tunjuk foto, 1–2 mahasiswa membuat satu kalimat lengkap (verb + preposition).
MOT30′

Aksi vs posisi

  • Action: S + am/is/are + V-ing · The workers are loading boxes onto the truck.
  • State: S + am/is/are + V3 · The chairs are arranged in a circle.
  • Guided noticing (5 kalimat) → pakai Latihan kelas di bawah.
  • Contrast practice: ubah 3 kalimat aksi menjadi kalimat posisi (A man is stacking the boxes → The boxes are stacked near the door.). Koreksi pelafalan -ing dan V3.
JCA35′

Describe & Draw / Select (info gap)

  1. Pasangan A/B duduk saling membelakangi. A membaca kartu deskripsi rahasia; B menggambar atau memilih gambar 1–4.
  2. B boleh bertanya: “Is the chair next to the desk or in front of it?”
  3. 10 menit, lalu bandingkan: salah di deskripsi atau di pemahaman?
  4. Tukar peran dengan kartu B.
  5. Debrief 5′: 2–3 pasangan berbagi kalimat yang sulit dan cara memperbaikinya.
ICA10′

TOEIC Part 1 check

8 soal foto. Bacakan sekali (±150 kata/menit), jeda 5 detik. Mahasiswa pakai Listening Answer Sheet, bukan lembar soal. Refleksi: satu kalimat per soal yang salah (wrong verb form / wrong location / not in photo).

Latihan kelas: action atau state?

Tayangkan di layar. Kelas menjawab, Bapak ketuk pilihannya, penjelasan muncul otomatis. Soal 1–5 = guided noticing dari lesson plan.

Naskah narasi Part 1 (8 soal · ±6 menit)

Directions: For each question, you will look at a picture and hear four statements. Choose the statement that best describes what you see in the picture. The statements will not be printed, and you will hear them only one time.

  1. (A) The man is filing paper into a cabinet. (B) The man is operating a photocopier. (C) The paper is scattered on the floor. (D) The man is repairing the machine. → B
  2. (A) The chairs are being carried out of the room. (B) People are sitting in the chairs. (C) The chairs are arranged in a circle. (D) The chairs are stacked against the wall. → C
  3. (A) The woman is cleaning the floor. (B) The woman is inspecting the equipment. (C) The equipment is being delivered. (D) The woman is assembling a shelf. → B
  4. (A) Workers are loading the boxes onto a truck. (B) The boxes are stacked near the loading dock. (C) A forklift is moving the boxes. (D) The boxes are being opened. → B
  5. (A) The colleagues are shaking hands. (B) The laptops are being repaired. (C) One colleague is leaving the room. (D) The desk is being cleaned. → A
  6. (A) The monitors are being installed. (B) A technician is unplugging the monitors. (C) The monitors are mounted on the wall. (D) The wall is being painted. → C
  7. (A) The student is filing documents into a drawer. (B) The documents are scattered on the floor. (C) The drawer is being repaired. (D) The student is reading a book. → A
  8. (A) A worker is pushing the cart down the hallway. (B) The cart is parked near the door. (C) The door is being painted. (D) The supplies are being unloaded by two workers. → B
Kartu deskripsi JCA (A & B)

A: In the picture, a woman is standing next to a filing cabinet. She is inspecting a folder. Two more folders are stacked on top of the cabinet. A chair is positioned in front of the desk, and a laptop is placed at the center of the desk. A window is located behind the desk.

B: A group of people is working around wooden desks in an empty lecture hall. On the left side, three people are looking closely at a laptop, with another person standing beside them holding a binder. On the right, a second laptop, some snack bags, and papers are resting on top of a podium.
