Meeting 2 · TOEIC Part 2

Inquiring & Giving Direct Personal Information

Ask and answer Wh-questions quickly and correctly.

Language focus: listen for the first word

Question wordIt asks aboutA good answer
Wherea placeRight next to the copy room.
Whoa personMarisa is handling it.
When / What timea timeAt 9:30 sharp.
Whya reasonBecause the manager is traveling.
Howa way, or a qualityYou'll need a password from IT. / It went really well.

Part 2 traps

  • Wrong information category: a time for a Where question, a place for a Who question.
  • Repeated word: the answer repeats a word from the question but does not answer it.
  • Sound-alike: a word that sounds similar to a word in the question.
  • Yes/No answer to a Wh-question: a Wh-question can never be answered with “yes” or “no”.
